Our story

A small counter with a big love for coffee

We started with a single espresso machine, a stubborn belief that great coffee should never be rushed, and beans sourced from growers we know by name. Every cup is brewed to order — never sitting, never stale.

Today the counter is a little bigger, but the idea is the same: honest coffee, warm pastries, and a place where the regulars are greeted by name. Order ahead and your drink will be waiting, exactly how you like it.
