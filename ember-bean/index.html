Sourced slowly, roasted in small batches

We buy directly from farms we have visited, paying above fair-trade prices for ripe, hand-sorted cherries. Every lot is roasted in 12 kg batches and rested for three days before it reaches you, so what you taste is the farm, not the roaster.
